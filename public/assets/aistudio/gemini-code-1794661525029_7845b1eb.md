1. **Field Instant Checks (সাইটে তাৎক্ষণিক পরীক্ষা — ৫টি)**:

   - Include an interactive **Field Cement Lot Inspection Scorecard** that evaluates all 5 checks and outputs an instant Accept/Reject recommendation.
2. **Fineness of Cement by 90-Micron Sieve (ASTM C430 / IS 4031)**:
   - Formula: $$R (\%) = \frac{W_2}{W_1} \times 100$$ where $W_1$ = initial sample weight (e.g., $100\text{ g}$), $W_2$ = weight retained on $90\ \mu\text{m}$ (#170) sieve after 15 mins sieving.
   - Standard Limit Chart: $\le 10\%$ for OPC (Pass), $> 10\%$ indicates coarse grinding or moisture pre-hydration (Reject).
3. **Normal Consistency of Cement (Vicat Apparatus — ASTM C187)**:
   - Plunger diameter $10\text{ mm}$; penetration target $5\text{ to }7\text{ mm}$ from bottom of Vicat mold ($33\text{ to }35\text{ mm}$ from top).
   - Formula: $$P (\%) = \frac{W_{\text{water}}}{W_{\text{cement}}} \times 100$$ (Standard range: $26\% - 33\%$).
4. **Initial & Final Setting Time of Cement (Vicat Needle — ASTM C191 / BDS EN 197)**:
   - Water added = $0.85 \times P \times W_{\text{cement}}$.
   - Formula: $$\text{IST} = T_2 - T_1, \quad \text{FST} = T_3 - T_1$$ where $T_1$ = time water added, $T_2$ = time $1\text{ mm}^2$ needle fails to pierce $5\pm 0.5\text{ mm}$ from bottom, $T_3$ = time $5\text{ mm}$ annular attachment makes no impression.
   - Standard Chart: IST $\ge 45\text{ min}$ (ASTM C150) / $\ge 60\text{ min}$ (EN 197); FST $\le 375\text{ min}$ ($6.25\text{ hrs}$) or $\le 600\text{ min}$. Explain why IST $< 45\text{ min}$ is dangerous for transit/placing and FST $> 600\text{ min}$ delays formwork removal.
5. **Soundness Test by Le-Chatelier Apparatus (BS EN 196-3)**:
   - Formula: $$\text{Expansion} = L_2 - L_1\ (\text{mm})$$ where $L_1$ = indicator distance after 24h cold water bath, $L_2$ = distance after 3h boiling bath.
   - Standard Limit: $\le 10\text{ mm}$ (Pass); $> 10\text{ mm}$ indicates excess free lime ($\text{CaO}$) or magnesia ($\text{MgO}$) causing delayed cracking.
6. **Compressive Strength of Cement Mortar Cube (ASTM C109 / BS EN 196-1)**:

### Category 2: Fine & Coarse Aggregate Tests (বালি ও পাথর/খোয়া পরীক্ষা)
Include complete 10-section details & interactive calculators for:
1. **Field Silt & Clay Content in Sand — Graduated Jar / Cylinder Method (বালিতে পলি ও কাদার পরিমাণ পরীক্ষা)**:
   - Formula: $$\text{Silt Content (\%)} = \frac{V_{\text{silt}}}{V_{\text{sand}}} \times 100$$
   - Interpretation Chart: $\le 6\%$ by volume (or $\le 3\%$ by weight in lab wash test ASTM C117) = Pass for RCC; $6\% - 8\%$ = Wash required before concrete mixing; $> 8\%$ = Reject lot.
2. **Sieve Analysis & Fineness Modulus (FM) of Sand & Coarse Aggregate (ASTM C136)**:
   - Standard ASTM Sieves (#4, #8, #16, #30, #50, #100).
   - Formula: $$\text{FM} = \frac{\sum (\text{Cumulative \% Retained on Standard Sieves})}{100}$$
   - Interpretation Chart for Bangladesh Practice:
     - $\text{FM } 1.20 - 1.50$: Fine Local/Domar Sand (Plastering / ফিনিশিং প্লাস্টার).
     - $\text{FM } 1.50 - 1.80$: Medium Sand (Brick Masonry Mortar / গাঁথুনির মর্টার).
     - $\text{FM } 2.20 - 2.50$: Blended / Medium-Coarse Sand (General RCC).
     - $\text{FM } 2.50 - 3.00$: Coarse Sylhet Sand (High-Strength Structural RCC / কলাম-বিম-পাইল ঢালাই).
   - Interactive 6-sieve table calculator that computes Individual Retained %, Cumulative Retained %, Fineness Modulus (FM), and recommends exact suitability.
3. **Bulking of Sand Test (বালির স্ফীতি বা আয়তন বৃদ্ধি পরীক্ষা)**:
   - Formula: $$\text{Bulking (\%)} = \frac{H_1 - H_2}{H_2} \times 100$$ where $H_1$ = initial loose moist sand height, $H_2$ = saturated inundated sand height. Show how much extra sand volume must be added in site volume batching (Farrah/measurement box).
4. **Specific Gravity & Water Absorption of Coarse Aggregate — Stone Chips vs. Picked Jhama Brick Chips (ASTM C127)**:
   - Formulas:
     $$\text{Bulk Specific Gravity } (G_{sb}) = \frac{A}{B - C}, \quad \text{SSD Specific Gravity} = \frac{B}{B - C}$$
     $$\text{Water Absorption (\%)} = \frac{B - A}{A} \times 100$$
     where $A$ = oven-dry weight, $B$ = SSD weight in air, $C$ = submerged weight in water.
   - Interpretation Chart: Stone Chips (Pakur/Bholaganj) absorption $\le 1.5\%$ ($G_{sb} \approx 2.65 - 2.80$); 1st Class Jhama Brick Khoa absorption $\le 12\% - 15\%$ ($G_{sb} \approx 1.85 - 2.10$); $> 16\%$ = Reject for RCC.
5. **Aggregate Crushing Value (ACV), Impact Value (AIV) & Los Angeles (L.A.) Abrasion Value (ASTM C131 / BS 812)**:
   - Formula: $$\text{ACV / AIV / L.A. Abrasion (\%)} = \frac{W_2}{W_1} \times 100$$ where $W_1$ = total dry sample weight, $W_2$ = weight passing $2.36\text{ mm}$ (#8) sieve (for ACV/AIV) or $1.70\text{ mm}$ (#12) sieve (for L.A. Abrasion).
   - Interpretation Chart per BNBC-2020 / RHD / LGED: Wearing surface $\le 30\%$, Base course / RCC $\le 35\% - 40\%$, $> 40\%$ = Soft/Weak aggregate (Reject).

### Category 3: Brick & Concrete Hollow Block Tests (ইট ও ব্লক পরীক্ষা — BDS 208 / PWD)

1. **Field Instant Brick Quality Checks (সাইটে ইটের ৫টি তাৎক্ষণিক পরীক্ষা)**:
   - *Ringing Sound Test*, *1.2m Drop Test*, *Nail Scratch Hardness Test*, *Frog Mark & Homogeneous Structure Check*, and **20-Brick Cumulative Dimension Tolerance Check**:
     - BD Metric Standard ($240 \times 115 \times 70\text{ mm}$) -> 20 bricks cumulative: Length $4800 \pm 80\text{ mm}$, Width $2300 \pm 40\text{ mm}$, Height $1400 \pm 40\text{ mm}$.

2. **24-Hour Cold Water Absorption Test of Bricks (ইটের পানি শোষণ ক্ষমতা পরীক্ষা — ASTM C67 / BDS 208)**:
   - Formula: $$W_{\text{abs}} (\%) = \frac{W_2 - W_1}{W_1} \times 100$$ where $W_1$ = oven-dry weight, $W_2$ = 24-hr submerged SSD weight.
   - Interpretation Chart: Picked Jhama / Ceramic Bricks $\le 10\% - 12\%$; 1st Class Brick $\le 15\%$ (PWD strict) to $\le 20\%$ (max allowable); 2nd Class Brick $20\% - 22\%$; 3rd Class $> 22\%$ (Reject for structural/exterior masonry).
3. **Efflorescence (Lona) Test of Bricks (ইটের নোনা পরীক্ষা)**:
   - Classification Chart based on salt deposit area (`0% = Nil (নোনা মুক্ত)`, `< 10% = Slight (সামান্য)`, `10% - 50% = Moderate (মধ্যম)`, `> 50% = Heavy (অতিরিক্ত — পরিত্যাজ্য)`, `Powder/Flaking = Serious (মারাত্মক — সম্পূর্ণ বাতিল)`).
4. **Compressive / Crushing Strength of Bricks & Hollow Blocks (ASTM C67 / C140)**:
   - Formula: $$f_b = \frac{P_{\max}}{L \times W}\ (\text{MPa and psi})$$ (average of 5 specimens).
   - Interpretation Chart: Grade S / Heavy Duty $> 20\text{ MPa}$ ($2900\text{ psi}$); 1st Class Brick $\ge 10.5 - 15.0\text{ MPa}$ ($1500 - 2200\text{ psi}$); 2nd Class $\ge 7.0\text{ MPa}$ ($1000\text{ psi}$); Concrete Hollow Block (Load Bearing) $\ge 13.1\text{ MPa}$ net area.
