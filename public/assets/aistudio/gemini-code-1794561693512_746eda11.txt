### 1. Module A: `rc-column-design` (RC Column $N\text{--}M$ Interaction)
- **Schema & Inputs:** Column width $b$ (mm), depth $h$ (mm), clear cover (mm), unsupported length $L_u$ (m), end restraint factor $k$ (braced/unbraced), concrete grade $f'_c$ (MPa), rebar yield $f_y$ (MPa), longitudinal bar diameter & arrangement (e.g., 8-$\phi$20mm or 12-$\phi$25mm), tie diameter/spacing, and factored loads $(P_u, M_{ux}, M_{uy})$.
- **Strain-Compatibility Interaction Engine (`src/utils/rcColumnEngine.ts`):**
  - Sweep neutral axis depth $c$ from pure compression ($P_0, \phi P_{n,max}$) through balanced failure ($c_b, P_b, M_b$) to pure bending ($P_n = 0$) and pure tension ($P_t$).
  - Compute both **Nominal ($P_n\text{--}M_n$)** and **Design Factored ($\phi P_n\text{--}\phi M_n$)** interaction envelopes for both X and Y axes, plus Bresler's Load Contour check for biaxial bending.
  - Evaluate Slenderness ratio $\frac{k L_u}{r}$ and Moment Magnifier ($\delta_{ns}$) if slender.
  - Generate `handCalculationSteps` for balanced strain condition, Whitney stress block depth $a = \beta_1 c$, steel layer strains/forces, and DCR utilization.
- **Visualization:** Interactive SVG **$P\text{--}M$ Interaction Diagram** plotting Nominal Curve, Design $\phi$-Curve, Balanced Point, Pure Axial Cutoff, and the applied load point $(M_u, P_u)$ with Safe/Overloaded status.
- **Cross-Link:** Add a button to push the designed column reinforcement directly into `rebar-bbs` (`elementType: 'column'`).
