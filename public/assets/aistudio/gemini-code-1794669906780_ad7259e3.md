### 1. Category 9: Site Execution & Structural Inspection Verification (সাইট এক্সিকিউশন ও স্ট্রাকচারাল চেকলিস্ট)
Build interactive, weighted Pass/Fail inspection verifiers with bilingual explanations, tolerances, and live calculators for:
1. **Pre-Pour Formwork, Shuttering & Staging Inspection (শাটারিং, সেন্টারিং ও প্রপস যাচাই)**:
   - **Diagonal Squareness Check Calculator**: Given panel/room length $L$ and width $W$ (in `0'-0"` Feet-Inches or `m`), compute theoretical diagonal $D_{\text{theo}} = \sqrt{L^2 + W^2}$ and compare with measured diagonals $D_1, D_2$ (Permissible error $\le 3\text{ mm}$ or $1/8"$).
   - **Column/Wall Verticality (Plumb / ওলন) Check**: Permissible deviation $\le 6\text{ mm}$ per $3\text{ m}$ ($10'-0"$) height (ACI 117 / BNBC).
   - **Slab Camber & Prop Spacing Check**: Upward camber for spans $> 4.5\text{ m}$ ($15'-0"$) = $L / 300$ to $L / 500$; Bamboo/Steel prop spacing, bracing at mid-height, and foam-tape watertight joint check.
2. **Pre-Pour Reinforcement & BNBC-2020 Seismic Ductile Detailing Check (রড বাঁধাই ও ভূমিকম্প সহনশীল ডিটেইলিং যাচাই)**:
   - **Clear Cover Block Verifier**: Cast-against-earth Footing/Pile Cap = $75\text{ mm}$ ($3"$); Water Tank/Retaining Wall = $50\text{ mm}$ ($2"$); Column & Beam = $40\text{ mm}$ ($1.5"$); Slab & Stair Waist = $20\text{ mm}$ ($3/4"$).
   - **Column & Beam Lapping Zone & Lap Length ($L_d$) Calculator**:
     - Column lapping strictly in the middle half ($L_0 \text{ to } H - L_0$ where $L_0 = \max(h_{\text{col}}, H_{\text{clear}}/6, 450\text{ mm})$), maximum $50\%$ bars lapped at one section, staggered by $\ge 1.3 L_s$, with hoop spacing $\le \min(100\text{ mm}, 6 d_b)$ over the lap zone.
     - Beam top bar lapping strictly at mid-span; bottom bar lapping near supports ($2 d$ away from column face), never inside beam-column joints.
   - **135° Seismic Stirrup/Tie Hook Verifier**: Hook bend $135^\circ$ with extension $\ge \max(6 d_b, 75\text{ mm} / 3")$, alternated at corners, plus Chair Bar ($\ge 12\text{ mm } \varnothing$ @ $1.0\text{ m} / 3'-3"$ c/c) verification for top slab mesh.
3. **Post-Pour Curing & De-shuttering (Formwork Stripping) Minimum Time Verifier (কিউরিং ও শাটার খোলার নির্ধারিত সময় যাচাই — BNBC / PWD)**:
   - Vertical sides of Column, Beam & Wall: $24 - 48\text{ hours}$.
   - Slab soffit (props left under): $3 - 7\text{ days}$; Removal of slab props (Span $\le 4.5\text{ m}$): $14\text{ days}$; Span $> 4.5\text{ m}$: $21\text{ days}$.
   - Beam & Arch soffit props (Span $\le 6.0\text{ m}$): $14 - 21\text{ days}$; Span $> 6.0\text{ m}$ or Cantilever: $28\text{ days}$.
4. **Brick Masonry, Plaster & Tile Finishing QA/QC Verifier (গাঁথুনি, প্লাস্টার ও টাইলস যাচাই)**:
   - Brick pre-soaking, frog mark upward, max construction height $\le 1.5\text{ m}$ ($5'-0"$) per day, $10\text{ mm}$ mortar joint thickness, Chicken Wire Mesh ($20 - 24\text{ gauge}$) at RCC-brick junctions, plaster thickness ($6\text{ mm}$ ceiling, $12\text{ mm}$ internal, $15 - 20\text{ mm}$ external), and 2m straight-edge flatness tolerance ($\le 3\text{ mm}$).
